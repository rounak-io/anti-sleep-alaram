Anti-Sleep Alarm for Laptop Students (1st Year Engineering & CS)

1. Idea in One Line & Problem Statement

Problem Statement: Students frequently fall asleep or experience severe microsleeps during late-night laptop study and exam revision sessions, which severely wastes study time, leads to poor academic retention, and damages academic results.

Core Solution: The laptop's webcam monitors the user's face in real-time. If the student's eyes remain closed for longer than 2.0 to 3.0 seconds, a loud synthesized buzzer alarm activates immediately and continues buzzing until their eyes reopen.

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Formula:
EAR = ( ||p2 - p6|| + ||p3 - p5|| ) / ( 2 * ||p1 - p4|| )

Where p1, p4 are the outer and inner horizontal eye corner landmarks, and p2, p3, p5, p6 represent the top and bottom eyelid landmarks. When eyes close, the vertical distance drops towards zero while horizontal width remains constant, causing EAR to decrease rapidly.

4. Two Implementation Options

Option A: Software Only (Recommended)

Requires zero additional hardware. Completely free and runs directly on the laptop webcam using browser WebGL and Web Audio API.

  • Zero component cost
  • Instant deployment across OS platforms
  • Built-in buzzer via Web Audio API oscillators
Option B: Hardware Add-on (Bonus Marks)

Connect an Arduino Uno or ESP32 via USB Serial or Bluetooth with an external 5V piezoelectric buzzer and alert LED.

  • Hardware: Arduino Uno, Active Buzzer, Jumper Wires
  • Serial communication via Web Serial API: sends '1' on sleep, '0' on wake
  • Physical vibrating wristband or desk siren integration

5. Common Challenges & Real-World Solutions

Glasses or Low Lighting: Adjust threshold slider or use the built-in 3-second Calibration feature. Ensure your desk lamp illuminates the front of your face.
False Alarms During Normal Blinking: Normal human blinks last 100ms - 400ms. The application requires eyes to remain continuously closed for ≥ 2.0s before triggering audio.
Variable Eye Shapes: The personal calibration utility samples 90+ frames of your natural eyes-open state to determine your personalized EAR threshold dynamically.
